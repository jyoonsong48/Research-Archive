## `Estimating human age in forensic samples by analysis of telomere repeats`
Karlsson, A. O., Svensson, A., Marklund, A., & Holmlund, G. (2008). Estimating human age in forensic samples by analysis of telomere repeats. Forensic Science International. Genetics Supplement Series, 1(1), 569–571. https: //doi.org/10.1016/j.fsigss.2007.10.153
### `Keywords:`
Telomere shortening, Age estimation, Real-time PCR
### `Summary`
With real-time PCR, the correaltion between age and telomere shortening were researched: the result yielded standard error of approximately 22 years. Telomeres were longer in buccal epitelium tissues than in blood.
