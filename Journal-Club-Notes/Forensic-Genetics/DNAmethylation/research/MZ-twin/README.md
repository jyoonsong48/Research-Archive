## `Genome-wide DNA methylome profiling to differentiate monozygotic twins`
Cho, M.-J., Park, S.-J., Lee, H. Y., Park, J.-L., Lee, S. M., Kim, J.-Y., Lee, S.-Y., Kim, S.-Y., Jun, J. K., & Lee, S. D. (2025). Genome-wide DNA methylome profiling to differentiate monozygotic twins. Forensic Science International: Genetics, 79, 103307. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.fsigen.2025.103307
### `keywords:`
DNA methylation, Monozygotic twin, Forensic, EPIC BeadChip, Biomarker
### `Summary`
STR profiling is unable to distinguish monozygotic twins. DNA methylation can be used in twin differentiation: showed the highest correlation when placenta and/or amnion is shared. Further research on mixed sample is needed as the study used only blood samples.
