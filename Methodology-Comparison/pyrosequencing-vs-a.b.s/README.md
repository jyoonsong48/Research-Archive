## `Pyrosequencing vs. Amplicon Bisulfite Sequencing`
| Name | Coverage | Platform | Pros & Cons | Cost |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Pyrosequencing** | **Short-range**<br>(~200 bp, predefined CpGs) | **Qiagen PyroMark**<br>(Chemiluminescence-based pyrophosphate detection) | **(+)** High quantitative accuracy<br>**(-)** Homopolymer noise, short read length | **Low to Moderate**<br>(Cost-effective for small sample sizes) |
| **Amplicon BS-seq** | **Mid-range**<br>(Hundreds of bp, continuous CpGs) | **Illumina NGS**<br>(Sequencing-by-Synthesis) | **(+)** Multiplexing available (High-throughput)<br>**(-)** Initial running cost, PCR/Bisulfite bias (Needs controls) | **High (Initial setup)**<br>Cost per sample: Cheap |
